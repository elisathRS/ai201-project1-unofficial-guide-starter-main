Housing Near Miami Dade College: What to Expect

Miami Dade College enrolls 35,001 students, creating high demand for off-campus housing in Miami, FL. Most students move off-campus after their first or second year either to save money or gain more independence.

Rent near campus ranges from $1,250/mo for a studio to $1,900/mo for a 2-bedroom — with 3 and 4-bedroom shared units often bringing individual rent down to $800–$1200/person/mo.

The area is moderately priced — affordable compared to major metro areas, with a solid range of options for all budgets.

Student Housing Tips

Start Early

The best apartments near campus get leased 6–9 months before move-in. If you want August housing, start searching in January–February.

Read the Lease

Check: who pays utilities, guest policies, subletting rules, and what happens if a roommate leaves. Never sign anything you haven't fully read.

Choose Roommates Carefully

Split a 3–4 bedroom and each person's share drops by 30–40%. Use a roommate agreement to align on noise, guests, cleanliness, and bills.

FAQ — Apartments Near Miami Dade College

What is the cheapest housing near Miami Dade College?
The most affordable option is typically a studio at around $1,250/mo. Sharing a 3–4 bedroom apartment brings individual costs down to roughly $800–$1200/person.
What are the best student neighborhoods near Miami Dade College?
Top areas include Miami Heights, Downtown, College Hill, The Loop. Each has its own vibe — some are walkable and social, others are quieter and more affordable.
What is the average rent near Miami Dade College?
Expect $1,250/mo for a studio, $1,500/mo for a 1BR, and $1,900/mo for a 2BR. Prices vary by proximity to campus and building amenities.
Is it safe to live off-campus near Miami Dade College?
The neighborhoods immediately around campus tend to be well-lit and student-friendly. Always tour in person, check entry security, and read tenant reviews before signing a lease.
How far are apartments from Miami Dade College's campus?
Most student apartments are 0.3–1.5 miles away — a 5–20 minute walk. Bus routes allow students to live 2–3 miles away without needing a car.
