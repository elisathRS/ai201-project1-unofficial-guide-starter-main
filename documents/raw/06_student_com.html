    Where is Miami Dade College located?

    Miami Dade College (MDC) is located across several campuses in the vibrant and sun-kissed paradise of Miami, Florida, USA.

    Welcome to Miami, the “Magic City” that offers top-notch learning mixed with a lot of sun and fun. The irresistible allure of the iconic landmarks, sizzling nightlife, pristine beaches, and mouthwatering cuisine of the area surrounding Miami Dade College alone is enough to captivate any visitor.

    The South Florida city possesses an incredible blend of tropical beauty, cultural diversity, and non-stop excitement. It is a city of irresistible charm where an energetic community, artistic expressions, flavourful cuisines, and stunning beaches collide.

    Sun kissed adventures

    Living in Miami will provide plenty of opportunities to immerse in a world of sun-soaked adventures.

    The iconic South Beach is a haven for sun-seekers and party enthusiasts. Feel the sand between your toes and soak up the vibrant atmosphere as you stroll along Ocean Drive, lined with colourful art déco buildings and buzzing with outdoor cafes, trendy shops, and pulsating nightlife.

    Artistic marvels and cultural gems

    A melting pot of cultures, Miami’s art scene reflects this diversity in the most captivating way.

    Explore the world-renowned Wynwood Walls, a vibrant outdoor museum where street art comes to life in a kaleidoscope of colours and styles. Delve into the contemporary art scene at the Pérez Art Museum Miami (PAMM), a striking waterfront museum showcasing thought-provoking exhibitions and breathtaking views of Biscayne Bay.

    Miami is renowned for its unique blend of Latin flair, and Little Havana is a symbol of that. Immerse yourself in the rich Cuban culture as you savour a cafecito, enjoy a flavourful cigar, and sway to the rhythmic beats of salsa music. Don’t miss the Calle Ocho Walk of Fame, honouring legendary Latin American artists, or the Domino Park, where locals gather for intense domino battles.

    Culinary delights

    A vibrant tapestry of flavours, Miami’s dining scene represents the city’s diverse cultural influences.

    Indulge in delectable Cuban sandwiches, mouthwatering seafood dishes, and tantalising fusion cuisine that marries Latin, Caribbean, and international flavours. Don’t forget to explore the vibrant food markets and food truck gatherings, where you can savour a medley of international delicacies in a lively and casual setting.

    Natural wonders

    World-renowned for its beautiful beaches, Miami also boasts a remarkable natural landscape that is as captivating as its vibrant cityscape.

    Explore the Everglades National Park, a sprawling wilderness just a short drive away, where you can spot alligators, majestic birds, and other fascinating wildlife on thrilling airboat rides.

    Venture further south to the Florida Keys, a chain of stunning islands, and discover the enchanting underwater world while snorkelling or diving in the crystal-clear waters of the coral reefs.

    For a taste of tranquillity, head to the Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden, a lush oasis filled with exotic plants, fragrant blooms, and peaceful paths that invite contemplation and relaxation. Take a leisurely stroll through the Miami Beach Botanical Garden, a serene sanctuary where nature’s beauty flourishes amidst the bustling city.

    Studying at Miami Dade College

    Miami Dade College (MDC) stands as a beacon of educational excellence, diversity, and innovation in South Florida. With its diverse community, exceptional academic programs, and support services, MDC offers a top-notch educational experience for anyone looking for an associate degree.

    From arts and humanities to business, health sciences, technology, and more, you’ll find a wide range of academic programs to suit your interests and career goals at MDC.

    State-of-the-art facilities

    MDC provides top-notch facilities that provide an optimal learning environment.

    From modern classrooms and laboratories to cutting-edge technology and library resources, the MDC campuses boast state-of-the-art facilities to ensure its students have all the tools they need to excel academically.

    Diversity

    Miami is known for its vibrant melting pot of cultures, and MDC perfectly reflects this rich mix.

    With a student body consisting of individuals from around the United States and the world, the college remains committed to diversity and inclusion. Miami Dade Community College has a higher number of minority students than any other college in the United States.

    In 2017 Dade was selected to be a member of the Frontier Set, a group of higher education institutions aiming to eliminate the racial, ethnic, and socio-economic barriers that prevent students from completing a college education.

    Recognitions and accolades

    Miami Dade is devoted to providing creative solutions that ensure students are on the right path toward employment. An example of this is the “Tesla START” program between the college and Tesla Corp., which trains students to become Tesla service technicians.

    The college’s commitment to these endeavours has been recognized over the years:

    In 2019, MDC received the prestigious Aspen Prize for College Excellence, the nation’s signature recognition of high achievement and performance among state colleges. Dade was recognized for its impressive retention and completion rates, its proactive student support services, and its impeccable record of improving students’ economic mobility.

    Also in 2019, Miami Dade was awarded the 2019 Lumina Foundation Education Innovation Judges Choice Prize. The college earned the top national distinction for its efforts in helping adult learners earn credentials that further their education and employability.

    In 2013, MDC was one of only seven colleges and universities in the USA to be honoured by University Business magazine as a Model of Efficiency for innovative approaches to streamlining higher education operations through technology and/or business process improvements.

    History of Miami Dade College

    Miami Dade College’s rich history is intertwined with the growth and development of the region. Let’s take a step back in time and explore the key milestones that have shaped the college into the esteemed institution it is today.

    1959 - Originally known as Dade County Junior College, Miami Dade College was established in 1959 with just over 1,400 students.

    1960s - MDC quickly outgrew its initial campus. Additional campuses such as The Wolfson Campus, North Campus, and Kendall Campus were established throughout Miami-Dade County to accommodate the increasing student population.

    1970s - As a response to the growing Hispanic population in South Florida, the college introduced bilingual education programs, ensuring that students from diverse backgrounds could pursue their educational dreams.

    1980s - MDC broadened its academic offerings to meet the changing needs of its students and the evolving workforce introducing new programs in emerging fields such as computer science, health sciences, and business administration. The college also established partnerships with local industries to provide students with hands-on training and valuable internships.

    1990s - As the world became increasingly interconnected during this period, Dade expanded its international education programs, fostering partnerships with universities and institutions worldwide to help prepare its students for an interconnected global society.

    2000s - The dawn of the 21st century brought forth a new era of technological advancements, and MDC was at the forefront of embracing that evolution. The college became a pioneer in e-learning, making education more accessible and flexible for students.

    2010s - The college implemented comprehensive support programs, including academic advising, tutoring services, and career development resources. It also established partnerships with local industries to provide students with internship and job placement opportunities, ensuring they are prepared for success in the workforce.
